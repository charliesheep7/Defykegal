Walk into any pharmacy and you will find a shelf of boxes promising harder erections and better performance. Almost none of it is medicine. In the United States there is exactly **one** over-the-counter product the FDA has authorised to treat erectile dysfunction, and it is not a pill.

Here is what is genuinely available without a prescription, what the rest of that shelf actually contains, and the approach with better outcome data than any of it.

## The One Real OTC ED Medicine

In June 2023 the FDA granted over-the-counter marketing authorisation to MED3000, sold under the name Eroxon. The [FDA announced it](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-roundup-june-13-2023) as the first over-the-counter treatment for erectile dysfunction cleared for sale in the US.

It is a topical gel, not a tablet. You apply it to the head of the penis and the volatile solvents in it evaporate rapidly, producing a cooling sensation followed by warming. That thermal stimulation of the nerve endings triggers nitric oxide release, which relaxes the smooth muscle inside the penis and lets blood flow in.

The numbers, as summarised by [Harvard Health](https://www.health.harvard.edu/mens-health/fda-approves-new-over-the-counter-gel-for-erectile-dysfunction):

- **About 65% of men** in clinical trials got an erection within 10 minutes and kept it long enough for sex
- **Faster onset** than sildenafil or tadalafil, which typically need around 30 minutes
- **Side effects in 1–3%**, mostly headache and nausea, with none reported in partners

Two honest limitations. It is a per-use aid — it does nothing for the underlying cause between uses. And it was studied largely in mild to moderate ED, so a man with severe vascular ED should not expect the same result.

## Everything Else on That Shelf Is a Supplement

This is the part most articles skate over. In the US, dietary supplements do not need to prove they work before going on sale. A box can carry bold performance claims and contain nothing that does anything.

The bigger problem is what some of them do contain. The FDA runs a permanent [list of sexual enhancement products found to be tainted](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/medication-health-fraud-notifications/sexual-enhancement-and-energy-product-notifications) with undeclared prescription drugs. In 2025 the agency flagged a product sold online that lab analysis showed contained both sildenafil and tadalafil with neither on the label, and it was [recalled nationwide](https://www.fda.gov/safety/recalls-market-withdrawals-safety-alerts/stuffbynainax-llc-issues-voluntary-nationwide-recall-mr7-super-700000-dietary-supplement-due). It was not an isolated case.

Why that matters beyond the deception: sildenafil interacts with nitrates. Men taking nitroglycerin or similar drugs for heart conditions can experience a dangerous drop in blood pressure. Those are exactly the men most likely to have ED and most likely to reach for something discreet off a shelf.

**The practical rule:** if a non-prescription pill produces a drug-like effect within an hour, assume it contains a drug. That is the most common explanation, not a botanical breakthrough.

## Why You Cannot Buy Viagra Over the Counter

Sildenafil and tadalafil remain prescription-only in the US, and the reason is screening rather than gatekeeping. A prescriber checks for nitrate use, uncontrolled cardiovascular disease, and certain eye conditions before writing it.

There is a second reason worth taking seriously. ED is frequently the first visible sign of vascular disease — the penile arteries are narrow and show damage earlier than the coronary arteries do. The [NIDDK](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/urologic-diseases/erectile-dysfunction/treatment) lists heart and blood vessel disease and diabetes among the leading physical causes. Buying something to mask the symptom skips a diagnosis that matters more than the erection.

Telehealth services have made prescriptions genuinely easy to get, which removes most of the reason men look for an over-the-counter workaround at all. ## The Non-Prescription Option With the Best Data

Here is the thing the pharmacy shelf cannot sell you: erections have a mechanical component, and it responds to training.

Blood arriving is only half of it. Keeping it there is the other half, and that job belongs to two muscles — the ischiocavernosus and bulbospongiosus — that wrap the base of the penis. When they contract they compress the crura and the deep dorsal vein, sealing blood inside the erectile tissue and pushing intracavernosal pressure far above what arterial inflow alone achieves. That is the difference between a filled erection and a rigid one.

Dorey and colleagues tested this directly in a randomised controlled trial published in *BJU International* in 2004, comparing pelvic floor muscle exercises against lifestyle advice in men with ED. At six months, **40% of the exercise group had regained normal erectile function and a further 35.5% had improved significantly**. A [2025 narrative review in the International Journal of Impotence Research](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41443-025-01034-5) reached similar conclusions across erectile and ejaculatory problems.

No over-the-counter pill in existence has produced numbers like that. The [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/erection-problems-erectile-dysfunction/) lists pelvic floor exercises among its standard self-help measures for erection problems.

## How to Choose, Realistically

1. **Mild, situational ED and you want something for tonight.** Eroxon is a legitimate option and the only over-the-counter one. Start training the pelvic floor in parallel so you are also fixing something.
2. **Consistent difficulty for more than three months.** See a doctor rather than shopping. Persistent ED is a cardiovascular flag, and a prescription is easier to get than most men assume.
3. **You want the underlying improvement, not a per-use aid.** Pelvic floor training plus the vascular basics — blood pressure, blood sugar, waist size, smoking, weekly alcohol. That combination has the outcome data.
4. **Never** buy a non-prescription pill claiming drug-like speed. Check the FDA tainted products list before anything goes in your mouth.

## What Progress Looks Like Without Pills

- **Weeks 2–4:** Contractions get easier to isolate and hold. Nothing visible yet, and that is normal.
- **Weeks 6–8:** Firmness starts to change. Morning erections are the most honest signal because they bypass psychology.
- **Week 12:** The trial timeline. Trained consistently, this is where the difference stops being ambiguous.
- **No change at 12 weeks:** Useful information. Book the appointment — something else is driving it.

## The Short Version

One over-the-counter ED medicine is real: Eroxon, a topical gel, FDA-authorised in 2023, roughly 65% effective within 10 minutes. Everything else marketed over the counter is an unreviewed supplement, and a meaningful fraction of that category has been caught containing hidden prescription drugs.
