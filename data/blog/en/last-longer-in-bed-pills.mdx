Search "last longer in bed pills" and you get two completely different categories of product wearing the same marketing language. One category has randomised controlled trials behind it and a prescription requirement. The other is a herbal capsule with a stock photo of a flexing torso, sold without a prescription, and there is a genuine chance it contains an undeclared dose of Viagra.

Knowing which is which is most of the value here. Here is what each type does, what the numbers actually show, and why the intervention with the best long-run data is not a pill at all.

## What These Pills Are Trying to Change

Ejaculation is a reflex with a threshold. Sensory input builds, and past a certain point the sympathetic nervous system fires the emission and expulsion phases and the outcome is no longer voluntary. The measurement researchers use is **intravaginal ejaculatory latency time (IELT)** — a stopwatch from penetration to ejaculation. Lifelong premature ejaculation is generally defined as an IELT under one minute.

There are only three levers a drug can pull:

- **Raise the reflex threshold centrally** — this is what serotonergic drugs do. More serotonin at the synapse, later trigger.
- **Reduce the sensory input** — topical anaesthetics, applied to the glans.
- **Alter the erection itself** — PDE5 inhibitors such as sildenafil, which help mainly when the underlying problem is erectile difficulty being rushed past.

Almost every legitimate product on the market is one of those three. Anything claiming a fourth mechanism is usually claiming nothing at all.

## The Prescription Options and Their Real Numbers

**Dapoxetine** is a short-acting SSRI designed specifically for on-demand use, approved in much of Europe and Asia but not in the United States. It has the largest evidence base of any oral treatment. An [updated systematic review and meta-analysis in 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32172793/) put the improvement in IELT at a weighted mean difference of about **1.67 minutes over placebo**, alongside better patient-reported control and satisfaction.

That number deserves a moment of honesty. If you currently last 40 seconds, dapoxetine takes you to roughly two and a half minutes. That is a real, meaningful change for many men — and it is not the transformation the advertising implies. Adverse events also ran at roughly double the placebo rate, mostly nausea, dizziness and headache.

**Off-label SSRIs** — paroxetine, sertraline, fluoxetine — are the standard approach where dapoxetine is unavailable. They are taken daily rather than on demand, take one to two weeks to reach effect, and carry the usual SSRI side effect profile including reduced libido, which is a real trade-off in this particular context. The [NHS lists antidepressants among the standard treatments](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/ejaculation-problems/) for ejaculation problems, alongside behavioural technique and topical anaesthetics.

**Topical lidocaine-prilocaine** is not a pill, but it belongs in the comparison because it frequently outperforms the pills. A [systematic review and meta-analysis of eleven randomised trials covering 2,008 men](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10474909/) found topical anaesthetics significantly more effective than placebo. In a head-to-head randomised trial, topical lidocaine spray beat on-demand dapoxetine 60 mg on latency. The trade-off is transfer of numbness to a partner without a condom, and reduced sensation for you.

**PDE5 inhibitors** are worth mentioning because so many "stamina" products are secretly made of them. Sildenafil and tadalafil do not directly delay ejaculation. They help when premature ejaculation is downstream of an erection that is not reliable — a pattern common enough that it is worth ruling out. [ED and PE overlap more than most men assume](/blog/how-to-stop-premature-ejaculation).

## The Over-the-Counter Category

This is where it goes wrong.

The FDA maintains a running list of [sexual enhancement products found to contain hidden drug ingredients](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/medication-health-fraud-notifications/sexual-enhancement-and-energy-product-notifications), and it runs to hundreds of entries. Independent testing has repeatedly found the same pattern: a majority of tested "natural" enhancement products contained an undeclared PDE5 inhibitor, most often sildenafil, sometimes tadalafil, sometimes both, at doses nobody measured.

Three specific problems follow from that:

1. **Nitrate interaction.** If you take nitroglycerin or any nitrate for a heart condition, undeclared sildenafil can drop your blood pressure to a dangerous level. This is the reason the warnings exist.
2. **Unknown dose.** Adulterated products are not manufactured to any standard. The capsule you take tonight and the one you take next week may contain very different amounts.
3. **It is not even the right drug.** PDE5 inhibitors do not delay ejaculation on their own. So the product is both illegally spiked and spiked with something that does not address the complaint.

The genuinely herbal ones — most of the market by count — largely do nothing. There is no supplement with trial evidence approaching what dapoxetine or a topical anaesthetic has produced. [Premature ejaculation pills](/blog/premature-ejaculation-pills) goes through the category in more detail.

## The Option That Does Not Wear Off

Pills change tonight. They change nothing about the underlying reflex, and the effect ends when the drug does.

Pelvic floor muscle training is the exception, because it changes the hardware. The bulbospongiosus and ischiocavernosus muscles are directly involved in the expulsion phase of ejaculation, and training them appears to give men measurable voluntary influence over a reflex they previously experienced as automatic.

Pastore and colleagues took 40 men with lifelong premature ejaculation and a baseline IELT under one minute, and put them through 12 weeks of pelvic floor muscle rehabilitation. Their results, published in *Therapeutic Advances in Urology* in 2014, showed [mean latency rising from 31.7 seconds to 146.2 seconds, with 82.5% of the men gaining control of the ejaculatory reflex](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24883105/). Of the men followed to six months, the improvement held.

A 4.6-fold increase compares favourably with anything in the drug literature — and the men who kept training kept the result. The catch is straightforward: it took 12 weeks of consistent daily work rather than one tablet an hour beforehand.

## How to Approach This Sensibly

If you want to be strategic rather than hopeful:

1. **Rule out the erection question first.** If firmness is unreliable, that is the thing to treat, and the ejaculation timing often follows.
2. **Start the training now, regardless.** It takes 8 to 12 weeks to show, so there is no reason to delay it while trying other things. [Kegels for men](/blog/kegels-for-men) covers finding and isolating the right muscle.
3. **Use behavioural technique in parallel.** The [stop-start method](/blog/stop-start-method-for-premature-ejaculation) and the squeeze technique cost nothing and stack with everything else.
4. **If you want pharmacology, see a doctor for it.** Prescription dapoxetine or an SSRI has known dosing and a known side effect profile. An anonymous capsule has neither.
5. **Skip the marketplace supplements entirely.** The upside is a placebo effect; the downside is an unmeasured dose of a cardiovascular drug.

## What Progress Actually Looks Like

With medication: an effect on the first dose for on-demand drugs, one to two weeks for daily SSRIs, and a return to baseline whenever you stop.

With training: little change in the first three to four weeks, noticeable ejaculatory control somewhere between weeks six and eight, and the substantial gains landing around the 12-week mark. Then it holds, provided you keep training at reduced volume. [How to last longer in bed](/blog/how-to-last-longer-in-bed) covers the full protocol.

The most sensible plan for most men is not either-or. Use something on-demand if you want relief tonight, and train the muscle so that in three months you do not need it.
