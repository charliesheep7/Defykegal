The hospital's [official profile of Dr. Lu Chong (陆崇)](https://www.gy120.net/dxshow.asp?articleid=3744), published in 2016, lists the roles of Associate Chief Physician, Associate Professor, and Director of the Health Examination Department. It describes work in medical oncology and, since 2009, health examination and preventive care.

That profile lists a Clinical Medicine bachelor's degree from Shanghai Railway Medical College, a 2009 master's degree in Integrated Chinese and Western Clinical Medicine from Guangzhou University of Chinese Medicine, and membership of a Guangzhou physician association health-management committee. It does not establish board certification in urology or a pelvic-floor specialty.
