The Defy Editorial Team writes guides on Kegel exercises, pelvic floor health, and men's wellness. We link to research and clinical guidance so readers can check the sources behind important claims.

An editorial byline is not a medical review. An article is identified as medically reviewed only when it displays a named reviewer and a review date. Articles without that label should not be treated as individually reviewed or as a substitute for personal medical advice.

Our editorial standards are documented in full on our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy) page.
